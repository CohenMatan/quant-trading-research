# X954 — Post-2010 survivorship-gap measurement (infrastructure)

Measures how many large US companies are missing from the MarketCap ≥ $2B universe because Morningstar has no fundamentals for securities that later ended. It also measures whether their omission biases returns up or down (D043).

It places no orders. Candidates come from the survivorship-free price universe.

Results are in `docs/data/survivorship_gap_2010.md`.
