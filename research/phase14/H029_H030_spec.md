## 2. Portfolio mechanics (frozen H026 / H028; identical for H028, H029, H030, LV15)

**Timing:**
- Review = the last session of each month.
- Scores are computed on the universe-selection day (the calendar day after the review session).
- Orders execute at the **next session's open**. Holdings run to the next review's close and are rebalanced at the following open.
- The 2021-11-30 decision is held to the 2021-12-31 close.

**Selection** (`qr_h026.pick` / `qr_h026.simulate`, unchanged):
- the **Top 15** by score, ties broken by security id;
- at most **3 per FF12 sector**;
- only stocks with a valid entry open;
- new positions at **equal target weight** (equity / 15 ≈ 6.7%, below the 10% initial maximum);
- existing holdings are not resized;
- cash when fewer than 15 qualify.

**Costs and capital:**
- $100,000 starting capital;
- **$7 per buy and per sell, 0.10% slippage per side**;
- long only, no leverage;
- delisted holdings follow the frozen alive / last-close rules.

**SPY:** total-return buy and hold over the same sessions, bought at the first entry open with $7 and 0.10% (`spy_simulate`).

## 4. H029 score (standalone)

**Universe:** U_H029(t) is the Data v3 universe (Data v2.1 eligible minus SIC 6000–6999 and no-SIC stocks).

**Signals:**
- The six Data v3 signals F1, F2, F4, F5, F7, F8 are computed live on the v3 view as known on the selection day (Data v3 spec section 5).
- The per-review H029 input-panel digest must equal the frozen Data v3 digests (131 / 131). Otherwise STOP.

**Score:** for each signal j, P_j = the centred percentile among the valid values in U_H029(t). The centred percentile is 2·(average rank − 1)/(n − 1) − 1, in [−1, +1], with "+" = improvement.

  H029 = mean of the stock's valid P_j

- At least 5 of the 6 signals must be valid; otherwise there is no score.
- Equal weights.
- No ML, λ, fitted weight, feature selection, interaction or winsorisation.

**Eligible set:** E29(t) = the stocks with an H029 score. The H029 portfolio is the Top-15 of E29(t) (section 2).
- **Sector:** the FF12 of the SEC SIC at the review.
- **Holding arrays:** the frozen `qr_h026.holding` construction on the same price panel.

## 5. H030 score (fixed 50/50)

**Joint universe:** J(t) = (H026 population at t, which has a valid H028 score) ∩ E29(t).

**Score:**

  H030 = 0.50 · centred(H028_raw over J(t)) + 0.50 · centred(H029 over J(t))

- No other transformation and **no other weight**.
- The H030 portfolio is the Top-15 of J(t) (section 2). Holding arrays and sectors are those of the H026 months.

## 7. Statistics (all strategies)

**Monthly returns:**
- Marks are taken at calendar month-ends of the daily equity paths (the last evaluation session of each calendar month), with the starting capital as the first mark (the `qr_h027.risk` convention).
- That gives 84 months, 2015-01 → 2021-12.

**Up / down months and captures:**
- A month is **SPY-up** if r_SPY > 0 and **SPY-down** if r_SPY < 0.
- **Up capture** = mean r_X over SPY-up months / mean r_SPY over SPY-up months.
- **Down capture** = the same over SPY-down months.

**Relative log return:** RL_t(X) = ln((1 + r_X,t)/(1 + r_SPY,t)).
- **S_UP(X)** = Σ over SPY-up months of RL_t(X).
- **Year contributions:**
  - U_y(X) = Σ RL over the SPY-up months of calendar year y;
  - A_y(X) = Σ RL over all months of year y.

**Wealth and risk:**
- **Terminal wealth** W after the final liquidation (`simulate` / `spy_simulate`).
- **CAGR, volatility, MaxDD:** from the frozen `qr_h026.path_metrics` on the daily path. Volatility is the annualised daily standard deviation; MaxDD includes the starting capital.
- **Beta:** monthly cov / var (ddof 1).

**Incremental:** S_INCREMENTAL_H030 = ln(W_H030 / W_H028).

## 8. Null (5,000 worlds; identity tether; H028 real)

**One world (seed s = 1 … 5,000):**
1. A fresh `qr_p7_pred.Tether(s)` (one stratum, persistent partners, no self-matching) steps over the 84 evaluation months in date order on the ids of E29(t).
2. Every recipient receives its donor's **complete six-signal vector**, including its missing pattern. The H029 score is recomputed from the permuted vectors (exactly section 4).
   - Because donors are themselves eligible, **E29(t), its size and every recipient's eligibility are unchanged**.
   - Returns, prices, corporate actions, sectors and H028 scores stay with the recipient.
3. The H029_null portfolio gives S_UP_null and the null H029 risk metrics.
4. H030_null = the section 5 blend of the **real** H028_raw and the permuted H029 score over J(t), giving S_INC_null = ln(W_H030_null / W_H028).

**Thresholds** (frozen `qr_h026.critical_value` convention):
- c_UP_H029 = the 50th largest S_UP_null of 5,000 (the 99th percentile).
- c_INCREMENTAL_H030 = the 50th largest S_INC_null.

**Empirical p** = (1 + #{null ≥ real}) / (1 + 5,000).

**Determinism:** the seeds 1–25 rerun must reproduce every recorded field exactly.

**Recorded per world:**
- for H029_null: S_UP, wealth, up / down capture, beta, volatility, MaxDD, name turnover, sector HHI;
- for H030_null: S_INC, wealth, up / down capture, beta, volatility, MaxDD, name turnover, sector HHI.

## 9. Null adequacy (before the thresholds are pinned; no real return used)

**A. Eligibility:** every null month has exactly the real E29(t) and J(t) sizes and memberships. This is checked in the canary.

**B. Selection structure (computed from scores only, without returns or holdings drift):** the per-month target list `pick(month, score)`.
- **Name turnover** = mean over months of |targets_t \ targets_{t−1}| / 15.
- **Sector concentration** = mean over months of the Herfindahl index of FF12 counts in the targets.
- For H029 and for H030, the real value of both measures must lie within the [0.5%, 99.5%] range of the 5,000 null values.

**C. Null risk distributions:** for H029_null and H030_null, volatility, beta, up capture and down capture must be finite in every world, with standard deviation > 0. The null medians of beta, up capture and down capture must lie in [0.5, 1.5].

**Outcome:**
- **Any failure → STOP — H029/H030 NULL INADEQUATE.** No other null is invented.
- **After the real run (descriptive only):** the real percentile of volatility, beta, up and down capture within the null.

## 12. Synthetic long-horizon robustness (ONLY if C1–C7 all pass)

**Method:** the P13-CP3 methodology (`research/phase13/cp3/P13_CP3_bootstrap.py`) on the paired monthly returns of H030, H028 and SPY from E031-05 (84 months, the section 7 marks).
- Every block is drawn jointly for all three series.
- Seeds use `default_rng(SeedSequence([method code, seed]))`.

**Variants:**
- **Primary:** 30 complete calendar-year blocks drawn with replacement; 10,000 worlds.
- **Sensitivities:** 12-month and 24-month moving blocks, 10,000 worlds each.
- **Stress:** year blocks from 2015, 2016, 2017, 2019 and 2021 only (2018 and 2020 excluded together); 10,000 worlds.

**Requirements (development gates, not probabilities of future success):**
- **primary:** P(W_H030 > W_SPY at 30 years) ≥ 0.90 **and** median W_H030 / W_SPY > 1;
- **stress:** median W_H030 / W_SPY > 1 **and** P(W_H030 > W_SPY) > 0.50.

**Patience metrics, H030 vs SPY and H028 vs SPY:**
- longest annual losing streak;
- relative MaxDD;
- longest relative-recovery duration;
- P(≥ 5) and P(≥ 10) consecutive losing years.
