## Hypothesis

The learned 11-feature Ridge score of H026 contains stock-selection information **beyond its LOWVOL exposure**, while still producing more terminal wealth than SPY after costs.

The real H027 portfolio is exactly the frozen H026 procedure. What changes is the question asked of it.

It is judged against a new null:
- LOWVOL keeps its real relationship with each stock;
- the other ten features (MOM, GPA, ACC, BM, AG, REV1M, CBOP, HI52, CFP, EQA) are replaced, as one vector, by a persistent tether donor's.

It is also judged against LV15_CONTROL, a Top-15 portfolio picked by LOWVOL alone.

## Why it might be true

- Profitability (GPA), proximity to the 52-week high (HI52) and other characteristics have pre-2017 published evidence of cross-sectional predictability that is distinct from low volatility.
- H026's coefficients on GPA, HI52, BM and REV1M were stable in sign across 2016–2021.

## Why it might be false

- The H026 post-mortem found the whole wealth advantage came in months when SPY fell, with beta 0.80 and down-capture 0.78. LOWVOL had a stable coefficient in every year.
- H026's unconditional null could not reproduce its low volatility. Once LOWVOL is kept real, the other features may add nothing.
- H022–H024 found no robust one-month information in these characteristics for 2011–2017.

## Motivation and its limits

- H027 is motivated by H026's already-seen 2015–2021 results (owner D213). It therefore cannot confirm anything on 2011–2021.
- No post-2017 market event motivates a rule. No crash, regime or trend filter is added.

## Kill criteria

- **Rejected:** any of A1–A6 fails.

- **Stop:** the real run does not reproduce H026.
