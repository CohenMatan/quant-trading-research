## Hypothesis

Some weighting `Score_v2 = wT·Tech/40 + wF·Fund/45 + wS·Sect/15` (`wT + wF + wS = 100`, on a pre-registered 5-point grid of 92 configurations) of the three frozen Score v1 layers ranks next-month total shareholder returns across the frozen Data v2 population. It must do so with search-adjusted significance, an economically meaningful 80+ group, monotone quintiles and stability over 2011–2017, inside a broad plateau of neighbouring weightings. That would justify one untouched validation on 2018–2021.

## Why it might be true

H022's diagnostics showed that the equal-information 40/45/15 combination is near zero. But the three layers measure different things: trend and momentum, quality, and sector participation. A component with negative or no predictive value can mask another with positive value.

## Why it might be false

- Every H022 view was null or negative:
  - IC near zero in every horizon;
  - sector-neutral and incremental views null;
  - 80+ group −12.6% a year.
- 12-1 momentum did not reproduce in this universe for 2011–2017 (H019).
- GP/A alone failed (H016).
- Searching 92 weightings manufactures apparent winners, so the search-level null must be beaten.
