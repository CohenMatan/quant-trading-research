# H007: Volatility contraction followed by expansion ("squeeze" breakout)

- **Status:** proposed 2026-09-29 for C02. Approved by the owner 2026-09-29 with the C02 plan.
- **Family:** volatility regime change (event-driven).
- **Strategy:** S007.

## Rationale

**Mechanism.**

- Volatility clusters and mean-reverts.
- A long quiet period (compressed range) often ends with a directional move, as information arrives and positions are rebuilt.
- Entering at the first expansion catches the start of the move with a tight, well-defined risk.

**Literature (pre-2018).**

- Volatility clustering and mean reversion: Engle (1982); Bollerslev (1986).
- Narrow-range and contraction breakouts: Crabel (1990); Bollinger (2001), the "squeeze".
- The academic evidence for contraction breakouts in single stocks is thinner than for H006. This hypothesis is partly practitioner-based, and that is disclosed.

## Rules

Signal at the T close; execution at the T+1 open.

**Setup: contraction on T−1.**

- v1.0: the 20-day Bollinger bandwidth ((4 × σ20) / SMA20) is in the lowest 10% of its own values over the previous 250 sessions.

**Trigger: expansion on T.** All must hold:

1. Close_T > the upper band (SMA20 + 2 × σ20).
2. True range_T ≥ 1.5 × ATR20.
3. Volume_T ≥ 1.2 × the 50-day average volume.

Conditions 2 and 3 define the *expansion* itself: the hypothesis is that range and participation expand when the contraction ends. A close above a tight band on an unexpanded range is not the event under test.

- The true-range baseline, ATR20, uses bars ending T−1.
- The volume baseline uses T−50 … T−1.
- H007 has **no takeover-specific rule**. Takeovers are handled only by the general data-integrity rules (D054, D062).

**Trend condition:** Close_T > SMA200.

**Ranking:** tightest contraction first (the lowest bandwidth percentile on T−1). Ties go by identifier.

**Exit (first to trigger):**

- close below SMA20 (the middle band), from the 5th session after entry on;
- time stop 40 sessions.

**Positions:** 10 slots, equal weight, daily scan and daily refill.

**Expected holding period:** about 10–30 sessions.

## Plateau perturbations (only if a variation passes)

- Contraction percentile 10% → 5 / 8 / 12 / 15%. For v1.1, the ATR ratio 0.6 → 0.3 / 0.48 / 0.72 / 0.9.
- Time stop 40 → 20 / 32 / 48 / 60.

## Costs

- The shorter hold means about 84–126 round trips a year at 10 slots and full exposure, which is **2.8–4.2% of equity a year**.
- This is the most cost-sensitive C02 idea after H009. The expected gross edge per trade must exceed about 0.34%.

## How it differs

| Compared with | Difference |
|---|---|
| H006 | H006 needs a new N-day high. H007 needs a *prior contraction* and can trigger anywhere in the range; its exit is the middle band, not a trailing ATR stop. The overlap of their trades is reported. |
| H005 | H005 *held* low-volatility stocks. H007 buys the *end* of low volatility. |
| H001 / H004 | Those bought weakness. |

## What result would falsify this idea?

- **Reject H007** if all three variations fail the IS screen.
- **Reject it also** if its best variation's average profit per trade is no higher than H006 v1.0's while trading more often. In that case contraction adds nothing to a plain breakout.
- The thresholds may not be changed afterwards.
