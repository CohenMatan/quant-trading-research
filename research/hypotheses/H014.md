# H014: Trend + pullback + confirmed recovery (Phase 2)

- **Status:** PROPOSED 2026-09-30 (`docs/checkpoints/P2_CP0_research_proposal.md`). Not approved, not implemented, never tested.
- **Programme:** P2, a new research programme separate from C01–C03.
- **Strategy:** S014 (proposed).

## Hypothesis

Among US common stocks with point-in-time market cap ≥ $2B that are in an established uptrend, buying after a temporary pullback **and** an objective recovery gives higher risk-adjusted returns, net of costs, over the following months than:

- buying the same uptrend stocks immediately (control C1);
- buying the pullback without waiting for recovery (control C2);
- random selection from the uptrend stocks (control R, 3 seeds).

## Rules (proposed, fixed by convention; none optimised)

- **Trend filter:** Close > MA200 **and** MA50 > MA200.
- **Pullback:** min RSI14 over T−5 … T−1 ≤ 40.
- **Recovery at T:** RSI14(T) > 45 **and** Close(T) > High(T−1); the trend filter still holds.
- **Execution:** signal after T's close; market-on-open at T+1.
- **Ranking:** 12-1 momentum, highest first; ties by security id.
- **Portfolio:** 12 slots at $100K; D051 cash rules; $7 per order; 10 bps slippage.

## Variants (2)

| Version | Exit |
|---|---|
| v1.0 | After 63 sessions, or earlier at the first close with Close < MA200. Horizon roll: kept if the stock would be re-bought that day. |
| v1.1 | At the first close with Close < MA200, or after 126 sessions at the latest |

## Falsification

H014 is rejected on the 2010–2021 development data if any of the proposed gates G1–G4 fails (`docs/checkpoints/P2_CP0b_revised_evaluation_methodology.md` §5):

- practical superiority over EW and SPY;
- beating the trend-only, no-recovery and random-uptrend controls;
- consistency across two-year blocks;
- robustness and costs.

If it passes and is frozen, it may go (with owner approval) to the one-time 2022–2026 Holdout under criteria HO1–HO3, and then to a forward test.

## Disclosure

Programme 1 tested related ideas on the same IS period:

- H001: oversold dips in uptrends, 10-day holds;
- H002: momentum;
- H004: momentum pullbacks, 10–20-day holds.

All were rejected, chiefly because of costs and weak performance relative to EW. H014 differs in:

- holding horizon and cost profile;
- the confirmed-recovery entry;
- the explicit controls.

It is not motivated by any post-2017 market event.
