# H022 — The frozen Conviction Score v1 ranks future stock returns

- **Phase:** 7 (Multi-Factor Stock Conviction Score).
- **Status:** pre-registered design (P7-CP4, owner D175). Not run. No real future return has been computed.
- **Owner:** decisions D165–D175.

## Hypothesis

Among the eligible US common stocks ≥ $2B (data-v1 universe, one share class per company, no hard disqualifier), stocks with a higher frozen Score v1 at a month-end review earn a higher total shareholder return over the following month than lower-scoring eligible stocks. In particular, the score-80+ group earns at least 3% a year more than the eligible average.

## Why it might be true (motivation written before any test)

The score combines three things only partly explained by each other:
- **Price trend and 12-1 momentum.** Tier A cross-sectional evidence before 2002; weaker since.
- **Profitability, cash conversion, balance-sheet strength and revenue growth.** Novy-Marx 2013 (gross profitability); Sloan 1996 (accruals); quality literature.
- **Participation of the stock's sector.**

The design requires breadth: no 80+ stock has a weak layer (P7-CP3 / CP3R). The hypothesis is that agreement between independent kinds of evidence identifies stocks whose subsequent returns are higher.

## Why it might be false

- Phases 1–6 found no robust edge in technical selection (H018, H019, H020) or in GP/A alone (H016).
- 12-1 momentum did not reproduce in this universe for 2011–2017 (H019).
- High scores are short-lived (median spell 1 month), so any edge must appear quickly.

## Exact test

`research/phase7/P7_predictive_spec.md`: a one-month Spearman rank IC, an identity-tethered null, gates G1–G4 and a single real evaluation.

## Not allowed

- Reweighting, removing or adding components.
- Score v2.
- Using another threshold or horizon after seeing results.
- 2018–2021 data (internal out-of-sample, later, with separate approval).
- The Holdout.
