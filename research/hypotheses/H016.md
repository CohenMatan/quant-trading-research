## Hypothesis

Among point-in-time eligible, non-financial, non-REIT US common stocks with market cap ≥ $2B and a computable gross profitability, the 20 stocks with the highest gross profits-to-assets earn a better risk-adjusted return than the equal-weight portfolio of that same universe, net of realistic costs. The portfolio is formed from information public at each quarterly rebalance, held to the next one, and equal weighted at entry.

The required size is the approved Phase 2 bar: Sharpe(H) − Sharpe(EW) ≥ +0.25, together with the rest of G1–G4.

## Economic rationale

- Gross profit (revenue − cost of goods sold) measures how productively a firm turns its assets into value before discretionary, investment-like expenses (R&D, advertising, sales effort) that GAAP expenses.
- With prices and book values fixed, a firm with higher expected profitability must carry a higher expected return (Fama & French 2006).
- Markets have historically under-priced the persistence of high gross profitability (Novy-Marx 2013).
- Scaling by assets keeps leverage out of the measure.

## Rule (all inputs from the frozen data infrastructure v1, `research/phase2/data_freeze_v1.json`)

**Measure on decision date T:**

  GP/A(T) = gross_profit_ttm4q(T) / total_assets(T)

- `gross_profit_ttm4q` is the True TTM: the four most recent visible consecutive quarters, Q4 validated.
- `total_assets` is the snapshot of the current point-in-time report, which must be the same fiscal quarter as the newest quarter of the TTM window. Otherwise GP/A is not computable.

**Validity:**
- total assets must exist and be > 0;
- negative gross profit is allowed (it ranks low);
- every input obeys the frozen point-in-time rules (availability after filing, +90 fallback, 200-day freshness, quarantine and field-level releases, restatement blocks, SEC timing holds).

**Universe at T** (identical for candidate, EW benchmark and controls):
- eligible under the harness rules (US common, NYSE/Nasdaq/AMEX, point-in-time market cap ≥ $2B, price ≥ $5, 20-day ADV ≥ $5M, SEC correction layer on);
- not financial/REIT under `qr_industry` (SEC SIC at filing, structure fallback);
- GP/A computable.

**Ranking:** GP/A, highest first; ties by security id, ascending.

**Schedule:** quarterly. The signal is taken at the close of the first trading day of March, June, September and December, and executed at the next open (harness market-on-open).

**Portfolio:**
- 20 slots, $100,000, equal slot weight at entry;
- D051 cash rules otherwise unchanged; $7 per order; 10 bps slippage;
- proposed minimum position $4,500 (owner decision).

**At each rebalance:**
- holdings not in the new top 20 are sold;
- new entrants are bought at the slot weight;
- continuing holdings are not resized.

**Between rebalances:** hold. No stop-loss and no exit on leaving the universe. Delistings are handled by the harness (data handling, not a strategy exit). Cash from acquisitions waits for the next rebalance.

**History-only warm-up:** from 2008-07-01 (D114). There are no decisions, orders or performance before 2010-01-04.

## What would falsify it

H016 fails if any gate G1–G4 fails over 2010–2021, with the H016-universe EW as the benchmark and the seeded random portfolios as controls. It is not re-specified afterwards.
