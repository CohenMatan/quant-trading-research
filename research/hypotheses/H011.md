# H011: Same-calendar-month return seasonality

- **Status:** proposed 2026-09-29 for C02; approved by the owner 2026-09-29 with the C02 plan (v1.0–v1.2 as declared below).
- **Family:** seasonality (cross-sectional, calendar-based). Unrelated to recent trend.
- **Strategy:** S011.

## Rationale

**Mechanism.** Stocks that did well in a given calendar month in past years tend to do well in that same month again. Suggested causes are recurring demand and supply:

- the timing of earnings and dividends;
- index and fund flows;
- tax and reporting cycles.

**Literature (pre-2018).**

- Heston & Sadka (2008): the effect persists up to 20 annual lags and is distinct from momentum and reversal.
- Heston & Sadka (2010): international evidence.
- Keloharju, Linnainmaa & Nyberg (2016): the same pattern in other asset classes.

## Rules

**Score, at the last close of each month, for the coming calendar month m.**

- Score = the stock's average return in month m over the previous L years, from adjusted monthly closes.
- All L annual observations are required.

**Portfolio.**

- Buy the top 10 at the next open, equal weight.
- Hold through the month, then re-rank.
- There is no keep band, because the ranking changes with each calendar month.

**Daily refill** from the current ranking, as in H008.

**Positions:** 10. **Rebalance:** monthly.

**Expected holding period:** about 1 month (21 sessions).

## Parameters

| Parameter | Base |
|---|---|
| L | 5 years |
| Holdings | top 10 |
| Weighting | equal |

## Data note (owner decision 2026-09-29)

- The signal uses only *past prices* as inputs.
- At the 2010 start, v1.0 reads prices from 2005–2009 and v1.1 from 2000–2009.
- **Approved by the owner only as signal warm-up / history.** Pre-2010 prices may not be used for parameter selection, hypothesis selection or tuning. Nothing is measured, compared or chosen on pre-2010 data.
- Universe eligibility (point-in-time MarketCap ≥ $2B) and the IS evaluation still begin on 2010-01-04.
- Stocks with fewer than L years of history cannot be scored. That excludes recent listings, which is a known bias and is disclosed.

## Timing

- The ranking is computed at the close of the **last session of month t−1**, from completed month-end closes only (lag 1 needs month t and month t−1 of the previous year, both long complete).
- Orders are next-open orders, so they execute at the **first open of month t**. Nothing executes at the ranking close.

## Plateau perturbations (only if a variation passes)

- L 5 → 3 / 4 / 6 / 7 years (or 10 → 5 / 8 / 12 / 15 for v1.1).
- Holdings 10 → 5 / 8 / 12 / 15.

## Costs

- The portfolio largely changes every month: about 100–120 round trips a year, **3.4–4% of equity a year**.
- The expected long-only excess return is about 0.5% a month (about 6% a year) before costs. **The margin is thin**; this is disclosed.

## How it differs

| Compared with | Difference |
|---|---|
| All other C01/C02 hypotheses | They all depend on recent price, volume or volatility. H011 depends only on the *same calendar month in past years*. Heston & Sadka show it is nearly uncorrelated with momentum. |

## What result would falsify this idea?

- **Reject H011** if all three variations fail the IS screen.
- **Reject it also** if the average monthly return of the chosen stocks is not above the equal-weight universe's in the same months (mean monthly excess return ≤ 0) for v1.0.
