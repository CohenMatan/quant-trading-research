# H003 — Proximity to the 52-week high

- **Status:** registered 2026-09-28, before any C01 data was examined.
- **Cycle:** C01.
- **Strategy:** S003.

**Hypothesis.** Stocks trading closest to their 52-week high outperform, because investors anchor on the high and under-react to good news near it.

**Evidence.** George & Hwang (2004); Li & Yu (2012).

**Prediction (IS).** The 15 names nearest their 52-week high (price / 252-day maximum close) beat the equal-weight universe risk-adjusted, net of costs.

**Risks.**

- Overlaps with momentum.
- May concentrate in low-volatility defensive names in calm markets.

**Variations (pre-declared, 3).** Each holds the top 15 at equal weight, with the same band as H002.
