## Hypothesis
At a weekly decision close, US stocks (≥ $2B) whose point-in-time chart shows a classical long setup score high on a fixed, deterministic 20-point checklist. The setup has four parts: weekly trend and structure, base quality, a fresh breakout trigger, and low, defined entry risk. Such stocks were expected to earn higher next-4-week total shareholder returns than low-scoring and disqualified stocks, beyond 12-1 momentum and "price above the 40-week MA".

**Rules of the score:**
- 20 binary conditions, 1 point each, plus 5 hard disqualifiers;
- no weights;
- no AI;
- no optimisation.
