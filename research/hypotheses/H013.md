# H013: Lottery-stock avoidance (extreme single-day positive moves)

- **Status:** PROPOSED 2026-09-30 for C03 (`docs/checkpoints/CP3e_C03_final_plan.md`). Not approved; not implemented.
- **Family:** avoidance of lottery-like stocks. **Strategy:** S013.

## Rationale (independent of H005 and of any Validation result)

- Investors with a preference for lottery-like payoffs (probability weighting, gambling preferences) overpay for stocks that recently had extreme positive one-day returns.
- Those stocks subsequently underperform. A long-only investor can capture this by **avoiding** them.

**Literature (pre-2018):**

- Barberis & Huang (2008), *AER*;
- Kumar (2009), *J. Finance*;
- Boyer, Mitton & Vorkink (2010), *RFS*;
- Bali, Cakici & Whitelaw (2011), *JFE*, the MAX effect, which persists after controlling for idiosyncratic volatility.

H013 never *selects* quiet or low-volatility stocks; it only excludes the upper tail of recent one-day jumps.

## Rules (fixed before any C03 result)

- **MAX** = a stock's largest daily simple return over the last 21 sessions ending at T, from adjusted closes. **MAX5** = the mean of its 5 largest daily returns in the same window.
- **Excluded set at T** = the eligible stocks in the top q of MAX (or MAX5) across all eligible stocks with at least 21 returns. Stocks with fewer returns are **not** excluded.
- **Portfolio:**
  - identical to the no-skill null X962 (15 slots, $100K, hold 60 sessions, daily refill);
  - the refill order is the null's own random order for (seed, session), over the **full** eligible set, with excluded names **skipped**.
- **Pairing:**
  - Wherever the null would buy a non-excluded name, H013 buys the same name in the same position of the order (common random numbers).
  - Where the null would buy an excluded name, H013 takes the next allowed name in the null's order.
  - After that, holdings and free slots can diverge naturally; the trade overlap with the null is reported.
- Seeds 1, 2 and 3 are paired with E962-22, E962-23 and E962-24.

## Evaluation

- Every seed is reported individually.
- A variation passes the IS screen only if **all three seeds pass**.
- DSR and PBO use the equal-weighted average of its 3 seed return series.
- Seeds are replicates of one candidate: counted once in the DSR N, and counted in the conservative count.

## Falsification

- **Reject H013** if no variation passes.
- **The avoidance effect is refuted** if the mean over seeds of [Sharpe(H013 seed k) − Sharpe(null seed k)] ≤ 0.05 for all variations, or if average profit per trade does not exceed the null's.
- No threshold, filter or exclusion level may change after results.
