# H009: High-volume return premium (volume shock without a large price move)

- **Status:** proposed 2026-09-29 for C02. Approved by the owner 2026-09-29 with the C02 plan.
- **Family:** volume anomaly (event-driven).
- **Strategy:** S009.

## Rationale

**Mechanism.** A burst of trading volume that is not explained by a large price move raises a stock's visibility.

- More investors notice and follow it, so the investor base widens.
- Because short-selling is limited, the newly interested buyers push the price up over the following weeks.

**Literature (pre-2018).**

- Gervais, Kaniel & Mingelgrin (2001), the high-volume return premium, over about a month.
- Investor recognition: Merton (1987); Miller (1977).
- International confirmation: Kaniel, Ozoguz & Starks (2012).
- Visibility effects also appear in Barber & Odean (2008).

## Rules

**Entry signal at the T close.**

1. Volume_T ≥ 2.5 × the median volume of T−50 … T−1.
2. |Close_T / Close_{T−1} − 1| ≤ 1.0 × ATR20%, with ATR20% from bars ending T−1.
   - This follows the original study, which separates the effect of volume from that of a contemporaneous return: the volume shock must not coincide with a large price move.
   - It is part of the hypothesis, not a takeover filter. H009 has no takeover-specific rule.
3. Close_T ≥ SMA50 × 0.9, which excludes stocks in collapse.

**Ranking:** volume ratio (Volume_T / median), highest first. Ties go by identifier.

**Exit:** time only, H sessions after entry.

**Positions:** 10 slots, equal weight, daily scan and daily refill.

**Expected holding period:** H.

## Pre-declared variations (3)

| Version | Change | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| v1.0 | One-day shock, hold 20 | The original one-month horizon |
| v1.1 | One-day shock, hold 40 | Whether the premium lasts longer; halves cost |
| v1.2 | Weekly formation: 5-day volume sum (T−4 … T) ≥ 2.5 × the median of the 50 previous rolling 5-day sums (those ending T−54 … T−5). The 5-day return must be within 1.0 × ATR20% × √5, with ATR from bars ending T−5. Hold 20. | The original paper's weekly definition |

## Plateau perturbations (only if a variation passes)

- Volume multiple 2.5 → 1.25 / 2.0 / 3.0 / 3.75.
- H 20 → 10 / 16 / 24 / 30 (or 40 → 20 / 32 / 48 / 60 for v1.1).

## Costs

- **Hold 20:** about 126 round trips a year, **about 4.2% of equity a year**.
- **Hold 40:** about 2.1% a year.
- The published premium is about 1% a month before costs. The strategy is therefore **highly cost-sensitive**; this is stated up front.

## What result would falsify this idea?

- **Reject H009** if all three variations fail the IS screen.
- **Reject it also** if the average profit per trade after costs is ≤ 0 for v1.0 and v1.2. The premium does not exist in our large-cap universe at our cost level.
- The volume multiple and the price cap may not be changed afterwards.
