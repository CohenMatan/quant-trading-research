## Hypothesis

Removing the annual λ selection and using one strong-shrinkage rule from the beginning (λ = 100 for every outer-year Ridge fit) makes the learned 11-feature score a more temporally stable stock-selection process. Specifically, it adds robust value beyond a simple LOWVOL portfolio while still beating SPY after costs.

- λ = 100 is a **pre-declared representative of strong shrinkage**. It is a value of the frozen H026/H027 grid, selected by H027 in 6 of 7 outer years.
- It is **not** claimed to be optimal, and no other value is ever tried.

## Why it might be true

- H027's 2015 model used λ = 0.01. Its coefficient vector was about an order of magnitude larger than the λ = 100 vectors of 2016–2021, which were stable in sign and size.
- 2015 is the year H027 lagged LV15 most (log-relative −0.171). A stable shrunk formula may simply not have made that year's large, noisy bets.

## Why it might be false

- The λ = 100 formula itself lagged LV15 in 2016 (−0.059). If the 2015 λ = 100 model also lags LV15, the 2015–2017 block stays negative.
- The instability may be deeper than the shrinkage hyperparameter (features, horizon or the market regime of 2015–2016).
- Because 2016–2021 reproduce H027 by construction (up to portfolio path dependence), any change in the verdict comes from 2015 alone. That is one year of already-seen data.

## Motivation and its limits

- H028 is motivated directly by H026/H027's already-seen 2015–2021 results (owner D217). It cannot validate, confirm or prove anything.
- No post-2017 market event motivates a rule. No regime, crash or trend filter is added.
