# H005 — Low-volatility anomaly

- **Status:** registered 2026-09-28, before any C01 data was examined.
- **Cycle:** C01.
- **Strategy:** S005.

**Hypothesis.** Low-volatility stocks earn higher risk-adjusted returns than high-volatility stocks.

**Mechanism.** Leverage constraints and a lottery preference make investors overpay for high-volatility names. Evidence: Ang, Hodrick, Xing & Zhang (2006); Baker, Bradley & Wurgler (2011); Frazzini & Pedersen (2014).

**Prediction (IS).**

- A 15-name low-volatility portfolio has a higher Sharpe than the equal-weight universe, net of costs.
- It may have a *lower* CAGR in a strong bull market. The Sharpe gate decides.

**Risks.**

- A bull period like 2010–17 favours high beta.
- The strategy is slow-moving, and at the edge of "swing trading" (holding periods of weeks to months). It is included as a defensive reference hypothesis.

**Variations (pre-declared, 3).** Each holds the 15 lowest-volatility names at equal weight, rebalanced monthly with the same band as H002.
