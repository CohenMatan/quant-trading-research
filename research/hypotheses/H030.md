## Hypothesis

Adding the independent Business Improvement signal (H029) to the frozen H028 learned score, with a **fixed 50/50 rank blend**, improves H028's terminal wealth and upside participation while keeping meaningful downside protection.

**The blend:** within the joint universe (stocks with both a valid H028 score and a valid H029 score), each score is mapped to its centred percentile in [−1, +1], and

  H030 = 0.50 × rank(H028) + 0.50 × rank(H029)

drives the same Top-15 portfolio.

**The weight is fixed:**
- 50/50 is a neutral first combination, not claimed optimal.
- **No other weight is ever tested under H030.**
- A different combination would be a new, owner-approved hypothesis.

## Why it might be true

- H028's edge came almost entirely from SPY-down months (up-capture 0.97, down-capture 0.69).
- An upside-oriented, uncorrelated signal could lift up-month participation while H028's half of the rank keeps the defensive tilt.

## Why it might be false

- Averaging ranks can dilute both signals.
- If H029 carries no information, H030 is H028 plus noise and loses wealth.
- If H029 is pro-cyclical, H030 may give up H028's downside protection (down-capture above 0.80).

## Motivation and its limits

- Gate C5 (excluding 2018 and 2020) is a stress **motivated by H028's observed regime dependence**. It is development evidence, not independent validation.
- H028 is never refit, retuned or modified: its frozen λ = 100 annual-fit score path is used as is.
