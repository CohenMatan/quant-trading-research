# H006: Breakout to a new N-day high on confirming volume (price expansion)

- **Status:** proposed 2026-09-29 for C02, before any C02 data was examined. Not yet approved.
- **Family:** breakout / price expansion (event-driven).
- **Strategy:** S006.

## Rationale

**Behavioural.** Investors anchor on salient reference prices (recent highs) and under-react to the news that pushes a stock through them. The price then drifts on after the breakout instead of jumping at once.

**Volume.** Heavy trading at the breakout signals informed, broad demand rather than noise.

**Literature (all pre-2018):**

- anchoring to highs: George & Hwang (2004);
- trend following and channel breakouts: Donchian (1960s); Faith, *Way of the Turtle* (2007); Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012);
- volume as information: Blume, Easley & O'Hara (1994); Gervais, Kaniel & Mingelgrin (2001);
- trailing (chandelier) exits: LeBeau & Lucas (1992).

## Rules

All signals are computed on completed day T with split/dividend-adjusted daily data. Orders execute at the T+1 open.

**Entry (all must hold at the T close):**

1. Close_T > the highest high of the previous N sessions (T−N … T−1).
2. Volume_T ≥ V × the average volume of the previous 50 sessions (T−50 … T−1).
3. Close_T > the 200-day simple moving average (uptrend context).
4. **Takeover-jump exclusion.** Skip the signal if Close_T / Close_{T−1} − 1 > 4 × ATR20%, where ATR20% is the 20-day average true range divided by the close. A takeover announcement jumps the price to the deal level and pins it there, which is not a breakout.

**Ranking** when there are more signals than free slots: breakout strength = (Close_T − prior N-day high) / ATR20, highest first. Ties go by security identifier.

**Exit (the first that triggers, signal at a close, sell at the next open):**

- **Chandelier stop:** Close < (highest close since entry) − K × ATR20.
- **Time stop:** 60 sessions after entry.

**Positions.** 10 slots, equal weight (10% cap, $5K minimum, the approved no-borrowing rules). Scanned daily; free slots are refilled daily.

**Expected holding period:** about 20–50 sessions.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Base |
|---|---|
| N | 55 |

| Trend filter | SMA200 |
| Jump exclusion | 4 × ATR20% |
| ATR length | 20 |
| K | 3.0 |

## Costs

- A round trip at about $9.8K per position costs about 0.34% ($14 commission + 20 bps slippage).
- About 63–84 round trips a year means **2.1–2.8% of equity a year at full exposure**. That is moderately cost-sensitive.

## What result would falsify this idea?

- **Reject H006** if v1.0 and v1.1 both fail the IS screen, and also have a profit factor below 1.2 or a lower 95% expectancy bound at or below 0. No new filters, stops or thresholds may be added afterwards.
- **The volume claim is falsified** if v1.2 (no volume) has a Sharpe at least as high as v1.0.
- **The anchoring claim is falsified** if v1.1 has a Sharpe no better than v1.0.
