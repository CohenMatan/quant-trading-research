# H002 — Intermediate-term (12-1) momentum

- **Status:** registered 2026-09-28, before any C01 data was examined.
- **Cycle:** C01.
- **Strategy:** S002.

**Hypothesis.** Stocks with the highest past 12-month returns, skipping the most recent month, continue to outperform over the next month or two.

**Mechanism.** Under-reaction to information, and gradual diffusion of news. Evidence: Jegadeesh & Titman (1993), Carhart (1997), Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013).

**Prediction (IS).** The top-momentum portfolio of 15 names beats the equal-weight ≥ $2B universe on a risk-adjusted basis, net of costs.

**Risks.**

- Momentum crashes in sharp market rebounds (Daniel & Moskowitz 2016; e.g. 2009, which is before IS).
- Concentration in 15 names increases variance.
- 2010–17 contained fast rotations.

**Variations (pre-declared, 4).** Each holds the top 15 at equal weight (≈ 6.5%). Rebalancing sells names that dropped out and buys new ones; kept positions are resized only outside a 25% band.
