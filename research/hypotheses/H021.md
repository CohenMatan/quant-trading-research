## Hypothesis (H021-A)
Among the nine original Select Sector SPDRs (XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLU, XLV, XLY), sectors with a higher trailing 6-month total shareholder return at a month-end close are expected to earn a higher total shareholder return over the next month, relative to the equal-weight average of the nine.

## Motivation (no post-2017 knowledge)
- Industry momentum: Moskowitz & Grinblatt (1999), with the 6-month formation and holding as the headline.
- The effect is strongest without a skip month (Grundy & Martin, 2001).
- Tradable ETF evidence: O'Neal (2000), and Andreu, Swinkels & Tjong-A-Tjoe (2013).
- Caution: the post-2000 iShares sector-ETF test was null (JAM 2014), and momentum decayed after 2000.

P6-CP1 recommended NO-GO, because the realistic effect (0–2.5%/yr) is below the detectable edge (≈ 3.3%/yr at 50% power). The owner chose to settle the question with this one test.

## Test
- **Data:** monthly decisions 2000-01 → 2017-11 (215; D035 amended for Phase 6 only).
- **Statistic and gates:** rank IC, with gates P1–P4.
  - P1: t_IC > c, where c is the 1% identity-tethered derangement null with R = 5,000, pinned before the real run.
  - P2: Top 3 ≥ +3%/yr.
  - P3: Top 3 > Middle 3 > Bottom 3.
  - P4: both halves positive, and no 6-year block above 50%.
- **Runs:** canary E989-01, null E022-01..05, then the real run E022-06 once.
