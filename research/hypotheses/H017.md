## Hypothesis

When a large US company releases earnings, and the market's reaction over the first two sessions after the information becomes publicly available is **strongly positive** (top decile of recent reactions), the stock continues to outperform over the next **60 trading sessions**. Investors under-react to the full implications of the earnings news (Bernard & Thomas 1989/1990; Chan, Jegadeesh & Lakonishok 1996; Brandt et al. 2008).

**Objective tested** (frozen Amendment 3): a continuous event-driven book of such positions finishes with more wealth than SPY buy-and-hold after costs, and beats the same-universe equal weight and matched random-event books.

## Distinct from H001–H016

- **Every earlier hypothesis conditioned on price or volume patterns alone** (reversal, momentum, 52-week high, pullback, low volatility, breakout, squeeze, residual strength, volume shock, gaps, seasonality, volatility management, MAX, trend + pullback), or on accounting ratios (GP/A).
- **H017 conditions on a verified information event** (an SEC 8-K Item 2.02 earnings release, timestamped by SEC acceptance).
- **The price reaction is measured only after that event**, as the market's interpretation of the news.
- **Evidence for the distinction** (Chan 2003; Savor 2012): informed moves continue, uninformed ones reverse. The price-only tests (H009 volume shock, H010 gaps) pooled both.

## Exact rules (proposed; frozen in a hash-pinned spec only after owner approval)

1. **Events.**
   - Source: Event Data v1 exactly as approved (P2-CP12 §19): SEC 8-K (original, not /A) with Item 2.02.
   - Timestamps: per-registrant time-zone conventions; first acceptance (US Eastern).
   - De-duplication: 30 days; amendments ignored.
   - Exclusions: foreign private issuers; ambiguous predecessor links, and contradicted or unverified identities (only CIK ↔ security links verified by dated ticker evidence or identity v2). The unique, time-disjoint predecessor links of the frozen rule are used.
   - No inferred dates.
2. **Event session E:**
   - BMO / during-market acceptance on session D → E = D;
   - after the close, or on a non-session day → E = the next session;
   - unknown time → as after the close of the filing date.
3. **Universe at decision:** the security is eligible in the frozen v1 harness universe (US common, point-in-time market cap ≥ $2B, price ≥ $5, ADV20 ≥ $5M, SEC correction layer) at the close of the decision session. Financial firms are **not** excluded: the signal is a market reaction, not an accounting ratio.
4. **Reaction (one definition):**
   - AR = [Close(E+1) / Close(E−1) − 1] − [SPY Close(E+1) / SPY Close(E−1) − 1], using adjusted prices (signals) as in the harness.
   - Close(E−1) is the last pre-availability price (the baseline). No price before availability enters the reaction itself.
   - Missing a close at E−1, E or E+1 → no signal.
5. **Strength threshold (one rule):**
   - AR ≥ the **90th percentile** of AR over all universe events whose decision session lies in the previous 252 sessions (strictly before today), and AR > 0.
   - At least 400 such events are required, otherwise no signals.
   - History-only warm-up from 2009-07-01, so events from October 2009, when the point-in-time universe begins, feed the first breakpoint. No trading before 2010-03-01.
6. **Decision and entry:**
   - Decision at the close of E+1.
   - Entry: market-on-open at E+2 (engine timing unchanged).
   - BMO events therefore enter at D+2; AMC events at D+3 (E = D+1).
7. **Volume:** not used (diagnostic only).
8. **Holding period:** exactly **60 sessions** from the entry open.
   - The exit order is placed at the close of the 59th session after entry, and executes at the open of session entry + 60.
   - No other exit, except the harness's forced exits (delisting / acquisition / 10 sessions without data).
9. **Same-company events while held:** ignored. No add, no reset of the clock.
10. **Portfolio:**
    - **N = 10 slots**; target slot value = 0.98 / 10 of current equity (≈ $9,800 at $100K).
    - Maximum position weight 10%; long-only; no leverage.
    - D051 settled-cash funding, 2% buffer and 15% gap reserve, unchanged. **No top-up**: in a rolling book the reserve rarely binds after the start.
    - Minimum new position $4,000 (reference minimum; rarely binding).
    - Costs: $7 per order; 10 bps slippage per side.
    - Accounts: $100K primary, $200K sensitivity only.
11. **Capacity:**
    - On each decision date, qualifying signals are ranked by **AR, highest first** (ties by security id).
    - They fill the slots that are free at that close (holdings after the day's exits, minus entries already pending).
    - Signals that do not fit are **dropped**: no queue, no replacement of existing positions.
12. **Window:** 2010-03-01 → 2021-12-31 (development); warm-up from 2009-07-01 (history only). Holdout locked.

## Controls (fixed before any run)

| Control | Definition | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| SPY buy-and-hold (E900-07) | Total return, the same dates | The objective (W1) |
| EW-H017 | Equal weight of the exact H017 universe (eligible ∩ verified domestic event filers), monthly, 25% band (B901 mechanics), $10M paper notional | Universe effect (W3) |
| Random-event books, seeds **1–5** | The identical code path, sizing, timing, holding and costs. On each decision date the book may open at most **k_t** new positions, where k_t = the number of the candidate's qualifying signals that day; they are chosen among **all** eligible events whose decision session is that day, by a fixed SHA-256 key of "seed\|security id\|event date" | Does selecting strong positive reactions beat taking random earnings events at the same moments? (W3: beat their median) |
| Canary book, seed 0 | As the random books, with canary checks | Infrastructure only |

## Diagnostics (pre-declared; never gates)

  - the mean and median for the top decile vs deciles 2–9 vs the bottom decile;
  - month-clustered t-statistics;
  - two-year blocks;
  - splits by timing class and by abnormal-volume tercile.
- DSR, PBO (if perturbations run), the Amendment 3 diagnostic set, the rolling 1/3/5/10-year reports vs SPY, EW and the random books.
