## Hypothesis

A linear combination of the 11 H024 stock characteristics (MOM, LOWVOL, GPA, ACC, BM, AG, REV1M, CBOP, HI52, CFP, EQA), with weights learned by Ridge regression only from information available at each decision date, ranks large US stocks well enough that a monthly Top-15 equal-weight long-only portfolio ends with more wealth than SPY buy-and-hold after realistic costs, without unacceptable extra risk.

Each yearly model is fitted on past months only. The labels are 63-session SPY-relative returns, used only once fully realised. The penalty λ is chosen by inner walk-forward portfolio wealth on earlier years only.

## Why it might be true

- Each characteristic family (momentum, low volatility, profitability, accruals, value, asset growth, short-term reversal, cash-based profitability, 52-week-high proximity, cash-flow yield, balance-sheet strength) has pre-2017 published evidence of cross-sectional return predictability.
- Combining many characteristics with past-estimated weights can predict returns better than any single one (e.g. Haugen and Baker 1996; Lewellen 2015, Fama-MacBeth forecasts from many characteristics). Shrinkage (Ridge) limits the estimation noise that defeats unregularised combinations.
- H023/H024 tested fixed or equal-weight combinations with a one-month IC statistic. H026 learns signed weights, targets a 3-month horizon and judges the portfolio by terminal wealth.

## Why it might be false

- H022, H023 and H024 found no robust one-month information in these characteristics for ≥ $2B US stocks in 2011–2017. A learned weighting of features without marginal signal cannot create one.
- Published anomalies weaken after publication and are smallest among large, liquid stocks (McLean and Pontiff 2016).
- A learned model can overfit the inner years. Year-to-year coefficient instability can make the portfolio a noisy factor bet that does not beat a strong 2015–2021 SPY.
- A 15-stock portfolio has high tracking error. Even a real edge of a few percent a year may not pass the null in seven years.

## Not motivated by post-2017 events

The features are the H024 set, chosen before any 2018+ data was read. The model class, λ grid, horizon, K and gates are the owner's D206 parameters. 2018–2021 is used as development data, and that is disclosed. No market event after 2017 motivated any rule.
