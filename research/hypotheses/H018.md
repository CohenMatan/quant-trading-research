## Hypothesis

Among 1,533 simple, pre-declared technical rules (one primary setup from trend, momentum, breakout or pullback; at most one confirmation from a different family; at most one volatility filter; 63-session holds, 10 slots), there is a **stable region of rules** whose stock selection grows wealth faster than SPY after realistic costs. The claim is that:
1. this is more than the best of 1,533 correlated tries would achieve by luck (the full-search permutation null);
2. it persists when the same procedure chooses only from earlier years (walk-forward 2014–2017);
3. it beats SPY, the same-universe equal weight and matched random books once on untouched 2018–2021 data.

**This is a hypothesis about the procedure, not about one hand-picked rule.** The tested object is the frozen search, including its selection step.

## Why it might be true (literature, before 2018)

- Trend and moving-average rules: Brock, Lakonishok & LeBaron 1992; Han, Yang & Zhou 2013.
- Cross-sectional and time-series momentum: Jegadeesh & Titman 1993; Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012.
- Nearness to the 52-week high: George & Hwang 2004.
- Short-term reversal inside longer holds: Jegadeesh 1990.

See `research/phase3/P3_literature_review.md`.

## Why it is likely to fail (stated before any result)

- **Weak published edges:** technical edges in liquid US large caps after costs are small, and many published ones shrink after publication (McLean & Pontiff 2016).
- **Low power:** the power calibration (P3-CP1 §26) shows that only a selection edge of about 6–8% a year above a random book has a realistic chance of passing.
- **Universe headwind:** the ≥ $2B equal-weight universe trailed SPY by about 4% a year in 2018–2021, a headwind for any stock-selection rule in the internal OOS.

**The most likely outcome is "No Production Candidate Found".**

## Frozen test (summary; the spec governs)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Q1 | The search statistic T (the plateau level of the best connected cluster of ≥ 3 eligible configurations) exceeds τ, the 25th largest of 500 within-date permutation null worlds (p ≤ 0.05) |
| Q2 | Procedure walk-forward 2014–2017: ≥ 3 picks; summed next-year log excess > 0 vs SPY and vs EW |
| Q3 | The ≤ 2 finalists are reproduced in a full LEAN harness run within the frozen fidelity tolerances; R4 holds |
| Q4 | Internal OOS 2018–2021, once: W1, W3, R1, R2, R4, g/SE ≥ 1.0, W1 at 2× slippage |
| Holdout | Only after owner approval (`HOLDOUT_UNLOCK.md`): HO-W and HO-R |
