# H010: Gap-and-hold (continuation after an overnight gap the market accepts)

- **Status:** proposed 2026-09-29 for C02. Approved by the owner 2026-09-29 with the C02 plan (timing per clarification 3 below).
- **Family:** gap / price structure (event-driven).
- **Strategy:** S010.

## Rationale

**Mechanism.**

- A large overnight gap usually reflects news. It is identified here from prices only; no news data is used.
- If the gap is *not* faded during the day (the close is at or above the open) and volume is heavy, the market has accepted the new price level.
- Investors typically under-react to such information, so prices keep drifting for weeks.
- Gaps that are faded tend to reverse, which this rule avoids.

**Literature (pre-2018).**

- Drift after price moves accompanied by information: Chan (2003).
- Under-reaction and post-announcement drift: Bernard & Thomas (1989).
- Drift after abnormal announcement-day returns (a price-based surprise measure): Brandt, Kishore, Santa-Clara & Venkatachalam (2008).
- Overnight versus intraday returns: Berkman, Koch, Tuttle & Zhang (2012); Lou, Polk & Skouras (SSRN draft 2015).

## Rules

**Timing (owner clarification 3, 2026-09-29).**

- The gap day is **T**. The signal uses T's open, T's close (the hold condition), T's low and T's volume, so it can only be evaluated **after day T is complete**, at T's close.
- The order is a market-on-open order for **T+1**. **Nothing executes on T.**
- Baselines use earlier bars only: ATR20% from bars ending T−1; the average volume over T−50 … T−1.

Enforced in code and tests:

- the harness allows orders only at the close and sends them as next-open orders;
- every fill is checked to be after its signal day and at the open price (`fills_after_signal_date`, `harness_timing_selfcheck`);
- `tests/test_c02_signals.py` proves the signal needs close_T, and that windows ending T−1 give no signal.

**Entry signal at the T close.**

1. Gap: Open_T / Close_{T−1} − 1 ≥ max(2%, 1.5 × ATR20%).
2. Hold: Close_T ≥ Open_T.
3. Volume_T ≥ 2 × the 50-day average (T−50 … T−1).
4. Close_T > SMA200 × 0.9, so the stock is not in long-term collapse.

*(Amended 2026-09-29, owner clarification 2.)* A draft cap of "gap ≤ 15%" was motivated by the takeover findings in the H005 Validation, so it has been **removed**. H010 has no takeover-specific rule. Takeovers are handled only by the general data-integrity rules (D054, D062).

**Ranking:** gap size in ATR units, highest first. Ties go by identifier.

**Exit (first to trigger):**

- **Invalidation:** close below the gap day's low (the gap has been filled).
  - The gap day is always the session before entry.
  - Its low is read from the split- and dividend-adjusted low window, so a split during the holding period cannot distort the comparison.
- **Time stop:** H sessions.

**Positions:** 10 slots, equal weight, daily scan and daily refill.

**Expected holding period:** up to H. Many trades end earlier on invalidation.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Base |
|---|---|
| Minimum gap | 2% and 1.5 × ATR20% (ATR from bars ending T−1) |
| Hold condition | on |
| Volume | 2× |
| Trend floor | SMA200 × 0.9 |
| H | 40 |
| Slots | 10 |

## Pre-declared variations (3)

| Version | Change | What it tests |

| v1.0 | Base, H = 40 | The idea as stated |
| v1.1 | H = 60 | Longer drift horizon, lower cost |
| v1.2 | No hold condition (close ≥ open not required) | Whether market "acceptance" is the key ingredient |

## Plateau perturbations (only if a variation passes)

- Minimum gap in ATR units 1.5 → 0.75 / 1.2 / 1.8 / 2.25.
- H 40 → 20 / 32 / 48 / 60 (or 60 → 30 / 48 / 72 / 90 for v1.1).

## Costs

- About 63 round trips a year at full use, or fewer if signals are scarce: **about 2% of equity a year**.
- Gap fills cut trades short and add turnover; this is disclosed.

## What result would falsify this idea?

- **Reject H010** if all three variations fail the IS screen.
- **The "acceptance" story is falsified** if v1.2 (no hold condition) performs as well as v1.0 or better.
- **Reject it also** if the average profit per trade is ≤ 0 after costs.
