# H015: Diversified low-turnover trend portfolio (Phase 2)

- **Status:** PROPOSED 2026-10-01 (`docs/checkpoints/P2_CP2_H015_preregistration_proposal.md`, D102). Not approved, not implemented, never tested.
- **Programme:** P2, hypothesis 2 of at most 3 before the Holdout is consumed.
- **Strategy:** S015 (proposed).

## Hypothesis

Among US common stocks with point-in-time market cap ≥ $2B, an equal-slot portfolio of 15 stocks has a higher Sharpe ratio, net of realistic costs, over 2010–2021 than the equal-weight eligible universe, SPY, and the same portfolio built without the trend filter. The portfolio is built as follows:

- The stocks are drawn only from those whose price is above their 200-day average at a monthly review.
- Each is held until a monthly review finds it below that average.

**The economic claim:**
- Stocks in an established long-term uptrend continue to do at least as well as the market for months (intermediate-horizon momentum, in its simplest binary form).
- Selling a stock when its long-term trend fails avoids part of the losses of stocks in sustained decline.

## Rules (proposed; one candidate)

**Review and universe**
- **Review:** the first close of each month, the same calendar as the EW benchmark.
- **Universe:** unchanged. Point-in-time market cap ≥ $2B, price ≥ $5, 20-day average dollar volume ≥ $5M; a real bar at the review close; at least 200 adjusted closes.

**Trend rule**
- Qualify when Close > SMA200, using adjusted closes at the review close.

**Exit**
- At a review, a held stock with Close ≤ SMA200 is sold at the next open.
- There is no time stop, stop-loss or rebalancing.
- The existing delisting and stale-data handling (D059) is data handling, not a strategy rule.

**Selection**
- Free slots are filled at the review from qualifying, not-held stocks.
- The order is a seeded random draw (X962 `pick_order` with the seed and the review date).
- Seeds 1–5 are replicates of one candidate, not candidates.

**Portfolio and execution**
- 15 equal slots at $100K; no leverage.
- D051 cash rules, $5,000 minimum new position, 10% weight cap at entry.
- $7 per order, 10 bps slippage.
- All orders are market-on-open at the next session.

## Falsification

Rejected on 2010–2021 if the development gates fail as approved (G1–G4, margin +0.25). G2 is adapted to H015's controls, as stated in the proposal §9 and §12.
