# H004 — Buying pullbacks in momentum leaders

- **Status:** registered 2026-09-28, before any C01 data was examined.
- **Cycle:** C01.
- **Strategy:** S004.

**Hypothesis.** Among stocks with strong 6-month momentum, a short sharp pullback is more likely temporary liquidity pressure than new bad information. Buying it combines the momentum drift (H002) with short-term reversal (H001).

**Evidence.** Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) on momentum; Lehmann (1990) and Jegadeesh (1990) on short-term reversal. The conditioning idea is in the spirit of Da, Liu & Schaumburg (2014), "A closer look at the short-term return reversal".

**Prediction (IS).** Entries beat the equal-weight universe, with positive expectancy net of about 0.42% round-trip costs.

**Risks.**

- Pullbacks in leaders can be the start of momentum crashes.
- Fewer signals, so higher variance.

**Variations (pre-declared, 4).**

- Leaders: the top 20% of eligible names by 126-day return, measured before the pullback window (t−131 → t−5).
- 15 slots; the deepest pullback enters first.
