# Owner message 2026-10-04: "Phase 4 — Deep Technical Indicator & Strategy Evidence Review — Evidence-First Research Before Any New Backtest"

This is a recorded summary; the full message is in the session transcript.

## Direction
- **Research and design only.** Before any new backtest, establish which technical concepts have enough empirical, academic and practitioner support, and what each one's correct **role** and **timeframe** is.
- The Phase 3 lesson (no-edge worlds always produce SPY-beating "best" configurations) raises the burden of evidence.
- Daily and Weekly are not assumed. A multi-timeframe answer (for example weekly trend + weekly ranking + daily entry + weekly exit) is acceptable if the evidence supports it.

## Concepts to review
- A trend (SMA / EMA, slope, filters)
- B cross-sectional momentum / relative strength
- C time-series momentum
- D 52-week high
- E breakout / Donchian
- F RSI
- G MACD
- H ADX
- I volatility
- J Bollinger Bands
- K volume
- L pullback / recovery
- M volatility contraction + breakout
- N multi-timeframe

## Required per concept
- What it measures; the evidence; horizon; overlaps; best role (primary / ranking / confirmation / entry timing / exit / risk filter / not used).
- Evidence tier: A (strong, replicated academic), B (credible but mixed or conditional), C (practitioner with some support), D (weak).
- Source hierarchy: peer-reviewed → working papers → quant books → institutional → reputable practitioner → forums (popularity only; no SEO, broker or marketing pages).
- Historical robustness (publication date, sample, replication, post-publication decay, non-US evidence, costs, small-cap concentration); large-cap relevance (≥ $2B); turnover class; redundancy; role ranking; timeframe and broad lookback ranges (no false precision).

## Strategy-building requirements
- Components should do different jobs.
- 5–10 canonical archetypes, each with: economic hypothesis, primary signal and confirmation, entry, exit, timeframe, holding period, turnover, failure regime, overlap with H001–H018, evidence strength, and whether it is distinct enough to test.
- Keep selection alpha separate from timing, and alpha separate from risk management.
- Assess common combinations: trend + momentum, trend + pullback, trend + volatility, breakout + volume, momentum + volatility scaling, weekly trend + daily timing.
- Compare Daily, Weekly and Multi-Timeframe on: noise, persistence, observations, turnover, costs, overfitting, entry precision, trend capture and $100K suitability.
- Classify each archetype against prior hypotheses; do not re-propose H014 or H018 under another name.
- Prefer a few strong hypotheses over a large optimizer.

## Deliverable
- **P4-CP2 — Deep Technical Indicator & Strategy Evidence Review** (33 items).
- Required tables: evidence matrix; overlap matrix; combination matrix; role and timeframe classification; exclusion list; compact toolkit.
- Final answers A–H.

## STOP conditions
- No backtest of any archetype.
- No final grids, no new optimizer.
- No 2018–2021 data, no Holdout.
- No production candidate, no H019.
- No purchase.
- No indicator returns computed on project data.
- Commit the report and source references, then wait for the owner's approval before designing the next experiment.
